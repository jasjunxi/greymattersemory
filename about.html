About Grey Matters

Grey Matters is a peer-reviewed, open-access interdisciplinary neuroscience journal dedicated to advancing our understanding of the nervous system. We publish cutting-edge research that bridges traditional neuroscience subdisciplines.

Our mission is to foster collaboration between molecular biologists, neuroscientists, psychologists, computer scientists, and clinicians—recognizing that the most important discoveries happen at disciplinary intersections.
