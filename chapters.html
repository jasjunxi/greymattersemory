Chapters

Grey Matters began at the University of Washington and continues to expand as a collaborative academic community.

The Grey Matters Journal was originally founded at the University of Washington with the goal of universality and accessibility in mind. Our mission is to create a global network of undergraduate neuroscience journals that champion collaborative, interdisciplinary research and make neuroscience accessible to students everywhere.
